Annotation guide

Each session is annotated in three stages. Every stage starts with a short instruction page, which you can reopen at any time with Instructions in the top bar.

Stage 1 · Read and segment

Read the whole transcript first. Then split it into phases: continuous stretches in which the therapist pursues one overall strategy (a macro action). To start a new phase, hover over the therapist turn where the strategy changes and click New phase here. Label every phase with the macro action that fits best. Use Merge with previous to undo a split.

Stage 2 · Annotate each phase

Phases are annotated one at a time, in two steps:

  1. Micro actions. Label every therapist turn in the phase with one micro action of the phase's macro action: the specific therapeutic move in that turn. Click a therapist turn, then pick an option. Choose None for minimal responses such as “Mm-hmm”.
  2. Phase reward. Judge how far the phase achieved the goal of its macro action: 0 Unsuccessful, 1 Acceptable or 2 Ideal, using the success levels shown.

Stage 3 · CTRS rating

Rate the therapist's performance over the whole session on the 11 items of the Cognitive Therapy Rating Scale (0–6). Anchors are provided for the even scores; use 1, 3 or 5 for performance between anchors. Then submit the session.

When nothing fits: Other

If none of the listed macro or micro actions is a good fit, choose Other and briefly describe what the therapist is doing. These descriptions help us improve the framework.
